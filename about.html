About SpecialTreat

We are available 24 hours. Just place your order and we will be at your door.

Our story

Cooked with care, served with a smile

SpecialTreat is a restaurant that makes everyday meals feel special. We cook familiar favourites such as fish and fries, hamburgers and cassava leaves, and pair them with coffee, cool drinks and yogurt.

We have trained and qualified chefs from different origins and cultures. Each brings their own skills and traditions, giving every plate a rich, balanced flavour that customers keep coming back for.

  • Available 24 hours a day for delivery requests.
  • Fresh ingredients and food cooked to order.
  • Simple ordering by phone, WhatsApp or our website.
